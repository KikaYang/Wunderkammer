# Wunderkammer - Interactive Museum Experience

An immersive, choice-driven digital narrative that transforms the Pinacoteca Nazionale di Bologna's Renaissance and Baroque paintings into an engaging, interactive journey through plague-era Bologna.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Wunderkammer reimagines the museum experience by gamifying art appreciation. Visitors step into the role of a citizen navigating Bologna during the 1630 plague, making meaningful choices that reveal historical context, artistic techniques, and human stories behind the museum's collection.

**Key Concept:** Instead of passively viewing paintings, visitors explore a branching narrative where they:
- Interact with historical NPCs (guards, gravediggers, priests, doctors)
- Collect artifacts that unlock new story paths
- Make choices that affect their emotional and knowledge journey
- Experience multiple endings based on their decisions

## 🏛️ Museum Context

**Location:** Pinacoteca Nazionale di Bologna, Italy  
**Collection:** Emilian painting spanning the 13th to 18th centuries  
**Star Assets:**
- Bologna Polyptych (Giotto, c. 1330)
- Madonna of Saint Margaret (Parmigianino, 1520-1530)
- Virgin with Child and Saints (Ludovico Carracci)
- Crucifixion (Titian, mature period)

## 🎮 Interactive Experience

### Core Mechanics
- **Branching Narrative:** Visitors make choices that affect their experience
- **NPC Interactions:** Dialogue with historically-grounded characters
- **Artifact Collection:** Items that unlock new story paths
- **Multiple Endings:** Based on empathy, knowledge, and choices
- **Visual Markers:** QR codes and AR markers trigger hidden content

### Thematic Layers
- **Historical Authenticity:** Content validated by museum curators and historians
- **Ethical Reflection:** Parallels between historical plague and modern pandemics
- **Multisensory Immersion:** Environmental sound design (bells, coughing, street noise)
- **Personal Embodiment:** Role-play as a citizen of 1630 Bologna

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

### Interactive Narrative
- **Twine/Ink:** Branching story scripting
- **Unity/WebGL:** Interactive scene rendering
- **CMS (Strapi):** Content management and localization

### Hardware Integration
- Touchscreen kiosks or tablets (≥10" display)
- Over-ear headphones for immersive audio
- Optional AR markers for smartphone interaction
- VR headset support for full immersion

### Digital Assets
- High-resolution painting scans
- 3D model of Piazza Maggiore (c. 1630)
- NPC portraits and animations
- Ambient soundscapes (bells, crowds, period-appropriate audio)

## 🎨 Design Principles

### Cognitive Engagement
- **Verification:** Expert-validated historical content
- **Perception:** Multi-sensory immersion through audio/visual design
- **Cognition:** Knowledge acquisition through discovery, not instruction
- **Personal Embodiment:** Physical and mental immersion

### Audience Experience
- **Familiarity:** Reconstruction using recognizable spatial cues
- **Interactivity:** Dynamic responses to user actions
- **Emotional Engagement:** Character-driven storytelling
- **Curiosity Triggers:** Hidden objects and optional interactions
